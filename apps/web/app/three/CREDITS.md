# 3D office style credits

The low-poly, fully-procedural visual style of this 3D office - the
rounded-box/cylinder primitives, procedural canvas textures, and the
box-built human figures with hair/skin/shirt/pants colors derived from
each agent's name - is adapted from:

**yugienugraha/ruang** — https://github.com/yugienugraha/ruang (MIT License)

Specifically ported/adapted from its `src/scene3d/props.tsx`,
`src/scene3d/textures.ts`, `src/agents.ts` and the `Character` component
in `src/pages/Office3D.tsx`. No model or image files are used anywhere in
this 3D scene, matching that project's own approach.
